• Use at least 12–16 characters. Prefer 20+ characters for important accounts. Use a mix of uppercase and lowercase letters. Include numbers. Include special characters such as ! @ # $ % &. Avoid using your name or username. Don't use your date of birth. Don't use your phone number. Don't use your email address or parts of it. Avoid common passwords like 123456, password, or qwerty. Don't use easily guessed phrases such as iloveyou. Avoid sequential characters, such as abcdef or 123456. Avoid repeated characters, such as aaaaaa or 111111. Don't use easily guessable substitutions like P@ssw0rd. Use a different password for every account. Don't reuse passwords from old or compromised accounts. Avoid information related to family, friends, pets, or hobbies. Don't include predictable patterns, such as Password@2026. Consider using a password manager to generate and store unique passwords. Enable two-factor authentication (2FA/MFA) wherever available—even a strong password can be compromised.
